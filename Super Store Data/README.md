## Overview
This project explores the Superstore dataset, a retail dataset containing sales, profit, and customer information across different regions, categories, and segments. The goal is to uncover insights into business performance and identify trends that can guide decision-making.

## Tools & Skills
Power BI (dashboard development and visualization) Power Query (data cleaning and preprocessing)

## Dataset
The dataset includes transactional records with key fields such as Order ID. Order Date/Ship Date, Customer ID, Segment, Region, Category/Sub-category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit and Product pricing tiers

## Process
Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand sales and profit distribution
Identify top-performing categories and regions
Detect loss-making segments and discount strategies
Build visualizations to communicate insight clearly

## Key Insights
The top highest selling product category and revenue falls between Technology, office supplies and Furniture
The West region contributes the highest sales but has lower profit margins
Home office segment has the lowest profit due to high discounts
